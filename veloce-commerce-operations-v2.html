Contract & Billing

Master Services Agreement & Payment

A plain-language summary of our terms. The signed Master Services Agreement is the governing document.

1. Scope of Services

Catalog protection, suppression triage and stockout monitoring for 15 to 30 agreed hero ASINs, plus a weekly review. Work outside this scope, including advertising, content design and software development, requires a written change order.

2. Fees & Billing

A flat retainer of USD 2,500 per month, invoiced monthly in advance for each milestone cycle. Work for a cycle begins once payment is received. Bank charges and any taxes are the client's responsibility unless the agreement states otherwise.

3. Service Levels

We target a mean time to resolution under 4 hours on covered listing defects. Resolution depends on Amazon's own processing, so outcomes controlled by Amazon are outside our direct control and sales results are not guaranteed.

4. Access, Confidentiality & Data

We use named, limited-permission accounts that you can revoke at any time. Your business data stays confidential and is used only to deliver the services.

5. Term, Termination & Liability

The agreement runs month to month and either party may end it with written notice as set out in the signed agreement. Liability is limited as stated in the signed agreement. Governing law and jurisdiction are specified in the signed agreement.
